Getting Started

How FinPass AI Works

Four simple steps to take control of your financial future

1

Sign Up & Profile

Create your account and provide basic financial information. Takes just 5 minutes!

Step 1 Instructions:

  • • Click "Get Started" on the landing page
  • • Enter your name, email, and create a secure password
  • • Fill in your age and employment status
  • • Click "Create Account" and verify your email
2

Financial Setup

Enter your income, expenses, and investment details to establish your financial baseline.

Step 2 Instructions:

  • • Go to the Wizard/Onboarding section
  • • Input your monthly income and fixed expenses
  • • Add details about your current investments and savings
  • • Complete the financial health assessment
3

Set Goals

Define your financial goals with timelines and amounts. AI will predict your success probability.

Step 3 Instructions:

  • • Navigate to "My Goals" in your dashboard
  • • Click "Add New Goal" and select category (retirement, home, education, etc.)
  • • Set target amount and desired timeline
  • • Review AI prediction for success rate and recommendations
4

Track & Optimize

Monitor progress in real-time and get AI-powered recommendations to optimize your wealth.

Step 4 Instructions:

  • • Visit your Dashboard regularly to check progress
  • • Review recommended actions from the AI Advisor
  • • Chat with the AI Financial Advisor for personalized guidance
  • • Update financial data quarterly to keep predictions accurate
